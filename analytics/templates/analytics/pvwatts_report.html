💡 Interpretation Guide
  • Bell-shaped curves: Normal daily irradiance patterns - expect steeper curves in summer, wider in winter
  • Scatter plot clustering: Tight clusters indicate consistent system performance; scattered points suggest variable conditions
  • Linearity: Power should increase linearly with irradiance up to system capacity
  • Seasonal shifts: Earlier peaks in summer, later peaks in winter due to sun position changes

⚡ Efficiency Metrics Explained
AC/DC Ratio

Measures inverter efficiency - typically 95-98% for modern inverters

Performance Ratio

Overall system efficiency compared to ideal conditions - good systems achieve 75-85%

Temperature Coefficient

Most panels lose 0.3-0.5% efficiency per °C above 25°C

Capacity Factor

Actual output vs maximum theoretical output - typically 15-25% for fixed systems

💡 Optimization Opportunities
  • Low-light performance: Check efficiency at low irradiance levels
  • Temperature sensitivity: Monitor efficiency drop with increasing module temperature
  • Seasonal variations: Identify if efficiency changes significantly between seasons
  • Daily patterns: Look for consistent efficiency throughout operational hours
